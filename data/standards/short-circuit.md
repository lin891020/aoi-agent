# WI-202 Short circuit — acceptance and disposition

## Definition
Unintended copper bridging two conductors that the artwork keeps separate.
Appears as added copper spanning the gap between two tracks.

## Classification
Critical. No short is acceptable at any product class.

## Disposition
Route to rework for excess copper removal, then re-inspect optically and
electrically. Boards shorted across a power plane are scrapped rather than
reworked.

## Re-verification notes
Shorts are visually distinctive -- added copper in a gap that should be clear --
and are among the most reliably classified defects. A confident short call
rarely needs escalation.

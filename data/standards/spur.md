# WI-204 Spur — acceptance and disposition

## Definition
An unintended protrusion of copper extending from a conductor into the
surrounding clearance, without reaching another conductor.

## Classification
Conditional. Acceptable when the remaining clearance to the nearest conductor
stays at or above 50% of the nominal spacing.

## Disposition
- Within limits: release.
- Outside limits: rework to remove the protrusion; a spur that closes most of a
  clearance is a short waiting to happen under humidity or bias.

## Re-verification notes
A spur and a short differ only by whether the copper reaches the far conductor.
When the model reports a spur with a competing short probability, treat the
clearance as unverified and escalate.

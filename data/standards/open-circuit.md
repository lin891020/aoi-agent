# WI-201 Open circuit — acceptance and disposition

## Definition
A break in the continuity of a conductor: the trace is interrupted so current
cannot pass. On a difference image it appears as missing copper along the run
of a track, usually narrow and elongated in the direction of the trace.

## Classification
Any confirmed open is a **critical defect**. There is no width or length below
which an open is acceptable, because continuity is binary.

## Disposition
- Confirmed open: scrap or route to rework for jumper repair, per the product's
  repair class. Class 3 product may not be jumper-repaired without customer
  concession.
- Suspected open that measures continuous on electrical test: record as a
  cosmetic thinning, release the board, and flag the artwork for review.

## Re-verification notes
Opens are the hardest class to separate from registration artefacts, because a
misaligned template also produces a narrow gap along a trace edge. When the
optical evidence is ambiguous, escalate rather than dismiss: an escaped open
reaches the customer as a dead board.

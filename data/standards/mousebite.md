# WI-203 Mousebite — acceptance and disposition

## Definition
A semicircular notch removed from the edge of a conductor, reducing its width
without breaking it. Named for the bitten appearance of the trace edge.

## Classification
Conditional. A mousebite is acceptable when the remaining conductor width is at
least 80% of the nominal width and the reduction does not extend for more than
two conductor widths along the run.

## Disposition
- Within limits: release, record for trend analysis.
- Outside limits: treat as a reduced-section defect and route to engineering
  disposition. Repeated mousebites on one panel indicate an etch or handling
  problem upstream.

## Re-verification notes
Because acceptance depends on remaining width rather than presence, a
mousebite call carries a measurement question the optical classifier does not
answer. Where the notch looks deep, escalate for measurement.
